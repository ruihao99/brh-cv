## Research Experience

### Quantum dynamics of open systems in condensed phases
Westlake University, Advisor: Prof. Wenjie Dou (Aug. 2022 – Jun. 2027 expected)

- Developed a tensorial extension of the **memory kernel coupling theory (MKCT)**, enabling generalized quantum master equations with general expectation values and cross-correlation functions for open quantum systems (*J. Chem. Theory Comput.* 2026).
- Derived a **universal moment-computing framework for exact quantum dynamics** with arbitrary system–bath couplings, with an open-source Fortran/MPI implementation (*J. Chem. Phys.* 2025, [code](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15393237)).
- Benchmarked **RI-CC2 for excited-state nonadiabatic dynamics** (ultrafast internal conversion in pyrazine) as a Q-Chem developer (arXiv 2026).
- Studied **spin-lattice relaxation with non-linear system–bath couplings**: Fermi's golden rule vs. extended dissipaton equation of motion (HEOM) (*J. Chem. Phys.* 2024); ongoing collaboration with experimental groups on **molecular spin-qubit relaxation** (spin–phonon polaron mechanism).
- Extended **electronic-friction theory at metal surfaces** by incorporating nuclear quantum effects via ring-polymer molecular dynamics (*J. Chem. Phys.* 2024).

### Machine learning accelerated molecular dynamics at electrochemical interfaces
Xiamen University, Advisor: Prof. Jun Cheng (Jun. 2021 – Jun. 2022)

- Trained **DeePMD** machine-learning potentials for the rutile TiO₂–water interface; revealed step-induced water dissociation and its double-row pattern (*Chem. Sci.* 2024) and the odd–even oscillation of surface dissociation (*J. Chem. Phys.* 2022, equal contribution).
